## Messaging Foundation (read this first)

**Site-level framework: April Dunford Positioning** — layered with a StoryBrand lens on trust/proof sections.

Why this framework: the entire strategic thesis of this rebuild is *positioning against two named competitive alternatives* — DIY marketing (scattered, inconsistent) and agency retainers (opaque, vague). The differentiation is specific and defensible: **clear, named, priced products you can see, buy, and stack.** That is a positioning problem, not an emotional-narrative problem, so April Dunford's "position against the alternatives, then make the unique value obvious" approach leads. StoryBrand supplies the secondary lens for trust/proof and for the industry pages, where the owner's transformation (scattered → systematic) and credibility carry more weight — especially Functional Medicine.

**Section-level formulas:**

| Section | Formula |
|---|---|
| Hero | BAB (stuck between DIY and agencies → a clear system you can buy) |
| Proof Stats | Pure social proof (big number + label + business) |
| Outcome-grouped catalog | FAB per Product Card (what it is + what it does for you + the outcome) |
| Signal tiers | AIDA |
| Calibration (setup) | Plain / objection-handling ("priced by checklist, not the hour") |
| Industry selector | Simple orientation |
| Testimonials | Pure social proof |
| FAQ | PAS reversal (name the objection, resolve it) |
| Closing CTA | AIDA (compressed) |

**Core narrative (keep in mind while writing every line):**
- The buyer is stuck between two bad options: do it themselves (inconsistent) or hire an agency (a vague retainer they can't see).
- We replace both with a system that's *visible* — every piece has a name, a price, and a plain "what you get."
- They don't have to buy everything. Start with one component ($97/mo), stack up to two, then graduate to the full system (Signal) when ready.
- Proof is concrete and outcome-first (owner.com-style stat cards), not adjectives.
- The audit is the on-ramp: a free 6-Point Marketing Audit that shows them exactly where they're leaking leads.

**Voice & tone:** Confident, precise, plain-spoken. Like a smart operator explaining exactly what you get and what it costs — no hype, no jargon, no fear-selling. Short sentences. Real numbers.

**This site must never sound like:** a generic agency ("we craft bespoke digital experiences to elevate your brand"), a fear-mongering funnel, or a feature dump. If a sentence could appear on any marketing agency's site, delete it.

## FAQ (PAS reversal — 5 questions)
1. **"Is this just another agency retainer?"** — No. Every piece has a name and a price. You see exactly what you're buying and can start with a single $97/mo component. No contracts on monthly components.
2. **"Do I have to buy the whole system?"** — No. Start with one component, stack up to two, and only move to Signal when you actually want the complete platform.
3. **"What does setup actually cost?"** — Setup (Calibration) is a one-time build priced from a standard checklist — most businesses land between $3,500 and $9,000. Anything you already have is removed from the quote.
4. **"Will this replace the software I already use?"** *(functional medicine / EHR)* — No. Signal runs alongside your existing tools and EHR — it adds the marketing layer around them.
5. **"What's the fastest way to see if this is worth it?"** — Take the free 6-Point Marketing Audit. It shows you exactly where you're leaking leads before you spend a dollar.
